Pixel and Plume Support

Pixel and Plume is a one tap arcade game by VibeDeV, published by VIBECORE DIGITAL. The same game is on Google Play as Tappy Bird. If something is not working, or you just have a question, we read every message.

Frequently Asked Questions

I bought Remove Ads or the Premium Pack. How do I get it back on a new device?

Those are non-consumable purchases, so they are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored at no extra cost. Open Shop and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Coin packs are consumable and are spent once used, so they are not restored.

I reinstalled the game and my coins and skins are gone.

Your progress, coins, unlocked skins, best score and settings are stored on your device only. We do not run servers and there is no account, which means there is no cloud copy to restore. Deleting the app deletes that data. Purchases are the exception: restore them as described above.

The leaderboard is empty, or my score is not showing.

Check that your device is signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app. A global leaderboard also stays sparse until an app has been publicly released and other players have posted scores, so a new or recently released game can look empty for a while even when everything is working. Your own score should appear once Game Center is signed in.

How do I change my ad or tracking choices?

  • Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. This controls whether the advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Personalised ads consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): in the game, Settings → Privacy Settings. That option only appears where consent is required.
  • Remove ads entirely: the Remove Ads purchase in the Shop clears the banner and the between game ads.

Do I need an account to play?

No. There is no sign up, no login and no personal details required. The game is fully playable offline. Game Center is optional and only affects leaderboards and achievements.

How do I reset my progress?

In the game, Settings → Reset Progress. This clears local game data. Purchases you have already made are kept, so you will not lose Remove Ads or the Premium Pack.
